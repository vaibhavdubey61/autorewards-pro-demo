# AutoRewards Pro — 5-Minute Client Demo Script

This script provides a structured, high-impact demonstration flow designed for stakeholder meetings, client pitches, and technical reviews.

---

### Segment 1: Landing & Partner Onboarding (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Action:** Open the live web app on the landing screen (`/#/login`).
- **Talking Points:**
  - *"Welcome to AutoRewards Pro — a specialized loyalty and warranty platform tailored for the automotive accessories market."*
  - Point out the clean mobile-first interface, role selector (Dealer vs Fitter), and one-tap demo shortcuts.
  - Highlight PWA compliance: partners can add this directly to their Android/iOS home screens with zero app store friction.

---

### Segment 2: Dealer Dashboard & Gamification (0:30 - 1:15)
- **Action:** Click **"Demo Dealer"** to log in as Arun Sharma (`9876543210`).
- **Talking Points:**
  - *"Arun is a car accessories shop owner in Mumbai. Notice his prominent AutoPoints balance card, Gold tier status, and 14-day streak counter."*
  - Review the visual tier progression bar and monthly scan target ring.
  - Demonstrate gamification: Tap **"Spin Now 🎡"**, run the interactive wheel, and watch bonus points trigger real-time confetti.

---

### Segment 3: Step 1 — Dealer Warranty Scan (1:15 - 2:15)
- **Action:** Navigate to **"Scan QR"** (`/#/scan`).
- **Talking Points:**
  - *"Here is the core differentiator: the dual-scan architecture. In traditional programs, coupons are lost or claimed by anyone. Here, the dealer must initiate Step 1."*
  - Tap **"Simulate Valid Step 1 QR Scan"** to load a pristine QR code (`ARP-QR-2026-001225`).
  - Show the customer details form (Customer Name, Mobile, Vehicle Make & Number).
  - Click **"Confirm & Claim Points"**:
    - Confetti fires celebrating +150 AutoPoints.
    - An official **Digital Warranty Certificate** is instantly generated.
    - Click **"Download Warranty Card PDF"** to demonstrate customer handover.

---

### Segment 4: Step 2 — Fitter Installation Scan (2:15 - 3:00)
- **Action:** Log out from Profile and click **"Demo Fitter"** to sign in as Raju Mistry (`9876543211`).
- **Talking Points:**
  - *"Raju is a car audio and electronics installer. When the customer drives into his bay, Raju scans the exact same QR code."*
  - Open **Scan QR** and input/simulate the unit. Show that Step 1 is verified as completed.
  - Raju confirms the installation date and can optionally attach an installation photo.
  - Tap **"Confirm & Claim Points"**: Raju receives his fitter reward (+100 AutoPoints).
  - Note rule enforcement: Show how scanning this code a third time immediately displays a friendly *"Already completed"* security notification.

---

### Segment 5: Wallet & Multi-Reward Catalogue (3:00 - 3:30)
- **Action:** Navigate to **"Wallet"** (`/#/wallet`).
- **Talking Points:**
  - *"Raju can review his ledger with 4 financial cards: Available Points, Lifetime Earned, Redeemed, and Pending."*
  - Tap **"Redeem Points"** to open the modal catalogue:
    - Amazon & Flipkart shopping vouchers.
    - Instant UPI transfer for immediate liquidity.
    - Mobile recharge plans.
    - Professional installer toolkit (Bosch, Stanley).
  - Submit a test redemption to demonstrate real-time ledger deduction.

---

### Segment 6: Competitive Leaderboard & Social Proof (3:30 - 4:00)
- **Action:** Navigate to **"Ranks"** (`/#/leaderboard`).
- **Talking Points:**
  - *"Technicians and dealers love competition. Here we see regional and national rankings."*
  - Point out the 3D Olympic podium display with Gold, Silver, and Bronze winners.
  - Toggle between Weekly, Monthly, and All-Time, or drill down by State (e.g. Maharashtra, Delhi, Karnataka).
  - Highlight the personalized overtake indicator: *"Only 85 points needed to overtake #5!"*

---

### Segment 7: Headquarters Admin Control Center (4:00 - 5:00)
- **Action:** Sign in as **Demo Admin** (`9999999999`) and go to `/#/admin`.
- **Talking Points:**
  - *"The manufacturer leadership team manages the entire ecosystem here."*
  - **Program KPIs:** Track partner growth, active scan velocity, total points liabilities, and estimated retail GMV.
  - **Batch QR Generator:** Generate a batch of 50 serialized QR codes and download the printable sticker PDF sheet.
  - **SKU Point Rules:** Adjust point incentives on high-margin or slow-moving accessories in real-time.
  - **Redemption Approvals:** Review and approve Raju's redemption request with 1 click.
  - **Live Scan Feed:** Observe real-time warranty registrations happening across India.

---

### Summary Close
- *"AutoRewards Pro turns passive warranty registration into an active, gamified dealer distribution engine. It builds direct brand loyalty with grassroots mechanics and protects your brand from gray-market counterfeiting."*
